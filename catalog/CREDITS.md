# Pictures in the catalogue

**Hero pictures and screenshots of games** are captures of the programs running on a PS2, made
with the games' demo or shareware data (Quake, Quake III Arena) or Freedoom (Doom).
**The shell's and the store's** are their own screens.

**Covers** (the tiles) are made of pictures that are in the public domain or CC0, from Wikimedia
Commons (the licence is that of the file page at the time of use; none needs a credit, they are
named here anyway):

|---|---|---|
| Quake | Gustave Doré, Dante lost in the dark forest (Inferno, 1866) | public domain |
| Quake II | NASA, SLS rocket stage moved from the Michoud Assembly Facility (2020) | public domain (NASA) |
| Quake III Arena, PS2 | Jean-Léon Gérôme, Pollice Verso (1872) | public domain |

| Duke Nukem 3D | NASA, Las Vegas at night from the ISS (iss073e0075845) | public domain (NASA) |
